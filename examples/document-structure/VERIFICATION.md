# Verification: From roll to block

Mandatory notes for this demonstration paper (`document-structure.trace.json`). Generated from the trace data on 2026-11-24, then checked by hand.

## How quotes were checked

- Every page below was fetched on 2026-11-24 with `curl` (raw HTML saved to the session scratchpad, tags stripped, whitespace collapsed). The Engelbart 1962 page was also read with WebFetch. WebFetch returns a model summary rather than raw text, so the raw `curl` copy was used to confirm each quote character for character.
- A script checked every `Exact quote` as an exact substring of its page text (whitespace normalised only). Result: 31 of 31 quotes found.
- Quote locations were taken from the HTML paragraph that holds each quote and its nearest heading. No page numbers are given.
- No quote is longer than 25 words, and no source has more than 2 quotes.

- **Reproductions, not originals.** Bush was checked against the W3C-hosted HTML copy (The Atlantic refused automated access). Coombs et al. was checked against the xml.coverpages.org reproduction, not the CACM PDF. Both Engelbart texts come from the Doug Engelbart Institute web editions. Wording could differ slightly from the printed originals.
- **Paraphrase beyond the quote.** Some sentences use a fetched page for more than the recorded quote, because each source is limited to two quotes. Each of these citations has a `Relation to this sentence` note saying which part is paraphrased and from where. Cases: rubrics in red ink (Johnson), the shared-address use of page numbers (Dabhoiwala), the debate over the codex and the Egyptian evidence (O'Hogan), the causes and sample of the IMRaD study (Sollaci & Pereira), XML documents being conforming SGML (Bray et al.), selection by association (Bush), Notion naming Engelbart and Nelson, and the Princeton exhibition's East Asian and pothī sections.

- **Undated pages.** The Princeton exhibition, the Caius exhibition and the Royal Society page show no publication date and are cited as n.d. The TEI Guidelines are cited by the version fetched (P5 4.12.0, last updated 28 July 2026).
